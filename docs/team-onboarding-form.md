# Team Onboarding Form

Use this interview to populate the brain later. The harness setup should create
the structure first, then team members supply the actual knowledge.

## Person

- Name:
- Role:
- Team:
- Decisions you own:
- Workflows people ask you about:
- Primary collaborators:

## Knowledge To Add

- What should the company brain know about your area?
- What docs, SOPs, project notes, or templates already exist?
- What is stale, wrong, or duplicated today?
- What should agents never assume without asking you?
- Which notes should be reviewed every 30 days?

## Source Systems

For each source, answer:

- Tool:
- Exact account/workspace/channel/folder/org:
- Company-owned, customer-owned, shared, delegated personal, or private personal:
- Owner:
- Review owner:
- What can be captured:
- What must be excluded:
- Destination folder:
- Sensitive route if needed:

Common examples:

- shared Apollo/CRM workspace
- company Fireflies workspace
- selected Slack channels
- Google Workspace service account
- company GitHub org
- customer workspace for one customer brain
- personal Gmail/Calendar/Fireflies/Apollo, excluded by default

## Capture Decision

- Should this source be excluded, proposed, approved for staging only, or active?
- Should raw material be retained, and for how long?
- Should notes require human approval?
- Is auto-promotion ever allowed for this source?

## Review

- Who approves staged notes from your area?
- What would make a note unsafe to promote?
- What should route to `Restricted/`?
- What should stay out of the brain entirely?
