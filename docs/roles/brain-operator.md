# Brain Operator

The Brain Operator keeps the company brain useful and safe.

The operator can be a teammate, consultant, or AI agent.

## What To Say

```text
Run today's company brain check.
```

or:

```text
$brain-operator
```

## Daily Responsibilities

- connection check
- source registry check
- health score
- lint
- staged proposal queue
- punch list

## Weekly Responsibilities

- stale-note review
- source ownership review
- restricted access review
- schedule review
- teammate onboarding follow-up

## What You Should Not Do

- do not promote notes without approval
- do not enable proposed sources by yourself
- do not inspect restricted folders
- do not treat warnings as facts without review
