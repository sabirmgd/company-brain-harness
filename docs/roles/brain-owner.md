# Brain Owner

The Brain Owner is accountable for trust.

## What You Approve

- where the brain lives
- who operates it
- capture policy
- source registry entries
- restricted folder access
- first schedule
- pilot launch

## What To Say

```text
I am the Brain Owner. Help me set up the company brain.
```

or:

```text
$brain-owner
```

## First Decisions

1. Company name.
2. Brain root location.
3. Brain Operator.
4. Restricted folder policy.
5. Human-gated or autonomous schedule.

Start with human-gated mode unless the team already trusts the workflow.

## Approval Rules

- Personal accounts are excluded by default.
- Shared company accounts can be proposed before activation.
- Raw transcripts and emails are not shared knowledge by default.
- Sensitive material stays restricted or out.
- Final promotion requires review during the pilot.
